Privacy Policy

Effective: 12 September 2026 · Last updated: 12 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how RizzKE ("RizzKE", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the RizzKE Android application and related services. RizzKE is an AI-powered social and dating conversation assistant. For privacy questions or requests, contact support.jengalabs@gmail.com.

1. Information we process

Depending on how you use RizzKE, we may process:

  • Content you choose to submit: text you paste or type, descriptions, screenshots you upload for analysis, and instructions about the help you want.
  • Saved app context: information you choose to save about people or conversations so RizzKE can provide more relevant suggestions, such as a person's name or label, relationship stage, notes, interaction history, and generated suggestions.
  • App and device information: a RizzKE device identifier and technical information needed to operate the service, enforce free-generation limits, troubleshoot problems, and maintain subscription access.
  • Usage and analytics information: events such as app sessions, screen views, onboarding completion, generation activity, paywall views, and purchase funnel events. We design our custom analytics not to include the text of your conversations, screenshot contents, saved people's names, or your RizzKE device identifier.
  • Subscription information: purchase tokens, product/base-plan identifiers, subscription status, and expiry information needed to verify access. Payment details such as your card number are handled by Google Play and are not provided to RizzKE.

2. How we use information

We use information to provide AI-generated replies and openers; remember context you ask RizzKE to save; operate and improve the app; enforce free usage limits; verify and restore subscriptions; detect and diagnose failures; understand product usage; and protect the service from abuse.

3. AI processing

When you request an AI generation, the content needed to answer your request may be transmitted to our backend and to our AI service provider, OpenAI, for processing. This can include text, screenshots, descriptions, and relevant saved context that you choose to use with the feature. Do not submit information you do not have the right to share, and avoid including unnecessary highly sensitive information.

4. Service providers

RizzKE uses service providers to operate the app. These include OpenAI for AI processing, Google/Firebase for app analytics and related mobile services, Google Play for subscription billing, Render for backend hosting, and Neon for backend database infrastructure. These providers may process information on our behalf according to their own terms and privacy practices.

5. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We may disclose information to service providers when necessary to operate RizzKE, comply with law or valid legal process, protect users or the service, investigate fraud or abuse, or as part of a business transfer such as a merger or acquisition.

6. Data retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including providing the service, maintaining subscription and usage records, resolving disputes, meeting legal obligations, and protecting RizzKE. Different categories of information may have different retention periods.

7. Deletion and your choices

RizzKE provides controls for managing saved people and app data. You may also request deletion of data associated with your RizzKE use by emailing support.jengalabs@gmail.com. Please include enough information for us to identify the relevant app data without sending passwords or unnecessary sensitive information. We may retain limited information where required by law, for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or legitimate recordkeeping.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information in transit and in our systems. No internet-connected service can guarantee absolute security, so you should avoid submitting information that is not necessary for the feature you are using.

9. Children's privacy

RizzKE is intended for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly provide the service to children under 18. If you believe a person under 18 has provided personal information through RizzKE, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

10. International processing

Our service providers may process information in countries other than your own. Data protection laws in those locations may differ from those where you live.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as RizzKE changes. We will update the date shown at the top of this page when we make changes. Material changes may also be communicated through the app or other appropriate means.

12. Contact

For privacy questions, requests, or concerns about RizzKE, email support.jengalabs@gmail.com.
