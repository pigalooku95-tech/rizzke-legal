RizzKE Child Safety Standards

Last updated: September 15, 2026

RizzKE is an AI-powered dating and conversation coaching application intended exclusively for adults aged 18 years and older.

RizzKE has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM). We are committed to maintaining an environment that protects children and complies with applicable child safety laws and regulations.

Prohibited Content and Conduct

RizzKE strictly prohibits any content or activity that:

  • Sexually exploits, abuses, or endangers a child.
  • Depicts or promotes child sexual abuse material (CSAM).
  • Sexualizes minors or encourages sexual interactions involving minors.
  • Facilitates or promotes grooming of minors for sexual purposes.
  • Facilitates sextortion, trafficking, or other sexual exploitation of minors.
  • Solicits, distributes, creates, or promotes sexual content involving minors.
  • Encourages or facilitates any other form of child sexual abuse or exploitation.

Age Requirement

RizzKE is designed for adults aged 18 and over. Individuals under the age of 18 are not permitted to use RizzKE.

Reporting Child Safety Concerns

If you become aware of content, behavior, or activity associated with RizzKE that may involve child sexual abuse, exploitation, grooming, CSAM, or the endangerment of a minor, please report it immediately.

Child safety reports can be sent to: support.jengalabs@gmail.com

Please include enough information for us to identify and investigate the concern. Do not send, forward, or attach suspected CSAM when making a report.

Our Response

RizzKE will review reported child safety concerns and take appropriate action when violations are identified. This may include restricting access to RizzKE and taking other measures necessary to protect users and minors.

Where required by applicable law, RizzKE will preserve relevant information and cooperate with appropriate law enforcement authorities or child protection organizations regarding suspected child sexual abuse or exploitation.

Compliance

RizzKE is committed to complying with applicable child safety laws and regulations and with Google Play's Child Safety Standards policy for social and dating applications.

Contact

For questions regarding these standards or child safety at RizzKE, contact: support.jengalabs@gmail.com
